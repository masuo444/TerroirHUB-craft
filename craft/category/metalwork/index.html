Japanese traditional metalwork encompasses casting, forging, and engraving across a range of metals. Nambu ironware from Iwate, Takaoka copperware from Toyama, Tsubame hammered copperware from Niigata, and Sakai cutlery from Osaka each exemplify different metalworking traditions.
