木竹工芸品

17品目

木材・竹・籐などを素材とした伝統工芸品。指物・挽物・曲物・建具・竹細工など多様な技法があり、日常の器具から美術工芸品まで幅広い品目が含まれます。

Japanese wood and bamboo crafts range from fine cabinet-making and woodturning to traditional basketry and lacquered woodwork. These crafts reflect a deep respect for natural materials and a centuries-old tradition of working with the grain and character of each piece of wood or bamboo.
